## Purpose

Each figure in the paper must say what it shows and does not show, and earn its place against a sentence. The supervisor's
direction (verbatim, `WP19-backlog.md`): "for each we need to be clear about what they do and do not show us. this is also related
to how/whether they earn their place in the paper." He is away; his instruction is "you should include candidate figures don't wait
on me ... get good initial candidate with clear purposes and at least the hypothesis that they adequately fulfill there purposes to be
inputs to a review and revise loop with me". The outcome: a slot list, a committed-code candidate for every slot that survives, each
placed in the paper after passing the figure review's loop, with a record per figure so that his review revises rather than starts.

## Goals

- G1. **Slots (stage 1).** For every section: the figures it has and could have, each with a one-sentence purpose, a "shows" line, a
  "does not show" line, and whether a sentence would do (then no slot). Evidence in: CR-4 of `WP11-round-1/triage.md` (Figure 2 helped
  6 of 6 readers and is the anchor; Figures 4 and 7 fail the cold read as drawn; the "w = ..." of Figures 5 and 6 needs a caption clause),
  the second review's finding that the three heatmaps are hard to read, `WP8a-figure-review.md`, and the ternary and diagram spikes.
- G2. **Candidates (stage 2).** For each surviving slot, a figure generated by committed code, taken through the figure review's loop
  (`docs/superpowers/notes/figure-review.md`) with the smallest change that makes it succeed.
- G3. **Placement (stage 3).** Each candidate that passes is placed in the paper, through the paper stream, with its caption and the
  three lines beside it (shows; does not show; what a reader would use it for).
- G4. **A record per figure** carrying PURPOSE, HYPOTHESIS that it serves that purpose, reader evidence, the three lines and the image
  path at the commit, so that his return review is a revise loop.

## Definition of good

- A paper whose every figure a reader of the matching persona and depth reads as intended from the image alone, with the caption adding
  only the clause it should; the n-lemma and the two model views make the method's structure visible where words alone were long;
  the supervisor's first look finds a purpose, a hypothesis and evidence for each figure and spends his time on taste, not on discovery.

## Definition of good enough

- D-1 complete (R-1, R-2); every slot that survived has a candidate that passed R-4 to R-9, or is marked dropped with its sentence;
  the two model views and the n-lemma ternary exist as candidates (placed if they pass, otherwise recorded as failed with the reads);
  CR-4 dispositioned (R-12); R-3 evidenced for every figure; R-13 and R-14 hold; `uv run pytest` and `uv run ruff check .` pass.
  Stop and present then: no further polish of figures that already convey their intent.

## Definition of done

Good enough is met, **no open item remains unresolved**, and the supervisor has accepted the work by the acceptance record above.

## Agent's defaults

- Slot list file name, record file names, and the 19a to 19e labels *(default)*.
- Two cold readers per figure (Sonnet subagents with no repository access; image alone, then caption) *(default)*; a third when the first two disagree.
- Persona choice by figure: P1 and P2 for the concept and case figures at L-A and L-B; P3 for the assembly and assessment views; P4 and P5 for the ternary and the tensions figures at L-C *(default)*.
- The 700 px PlantUML output and the paper's column width as the legibility width; light scheme judged first *(default)*.
- Model views in the paper are included in the paper's body only if they pass; otherwise they go to the model's documentation, with the reads recorded *(default)*.
- Hypotheses are written as one falsifiable sentence per figure ("a P2 reader at L-B names X from the image alone") *(default)*.
- Cost ordering within a subpackage: sentence test first, then recipe, then generation, then reads *(default)*.
