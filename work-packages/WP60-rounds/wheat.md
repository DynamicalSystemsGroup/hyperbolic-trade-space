# WP60 wheat page: the log-plane figure

Figure 8 of the docsite, `docs/examples/wheat.md`, drawn by the runnable block above it (the derived CSV, standard-scaled logs, `select_temperature` at a tenth of rows above the surface, `plot_log_plane` with band 1/tau). Images: `wheat-figure-light.png`, `wheat-figure-dark.png` (built by `scripts/build_figures.py`; the slow figure test checks that they are non-blank and differ).

**Sentence test.** The frontier is a falling straight line in log grain against log straw, with the plot-years beneath it and a thin grey band along it holding the nearest ones. The sentence is true of what is drawn.

**Cold read.** The cloud rises toward the upper right across the whole table (plots with more grain have more straw), while the frontier at the upper edge falls: the dilemma is at the edge, not in the cloud. The caption says the line is the frontier and the band holds the nearest plot-years, and claims nothing about the cloud. The long tail of low plot-years to the lower left is the table, not an outlier treatment.

**Ink as data.** Points, the line and the band only; the legend names the band's cut-off (log V at least -0.121) and the line (V = 1). Axis labels say standardized logs. Budget: eight figures, met (this is the eighth).

**Shows / does not show.** Shows the line, the band and where the plot-years lie. Does not show the second term (a grain ceiling at K = 2), the cultivar periods or the exponents' range; the text carries them.
