# WP23 round 1: the two views of `cubesat_r2.sysml`

Generated by `docs/superpowers/notes/spike-sysml-diagrams/project.py` (revision 3) from
`examples/adcs/model_v2/cubesat_r2.sysml`; rendered by its `render.sh` (PlantUML, 700 px wide). The
committed views of the adopted model (`examples/adcs/model_v2/views/`) are untouched until the data
gate; the generator gives them byte for byte. The intent of each view is written before the cold
read, as the sentence a reader should be able to say after one look.

## assembly_view.png

After one look a reader says: "a configuration is a cubesat of nine parts; two of them are swapped
for one of three alternatives (the wheel class, the tracker grade) and four numbers are tuned within
their ranges (panel span, battery capacity, the gains Kp and Kd); that is the whole configuration
space". The view is the same as the adopted model's: the revision changes how the configuration is
evaluated, not how it is constructed.

## behavioural_view.png

After one look a reader says: "every traded metric, in its subsystem, is an MoE or an MoP that one
requirement reads by its own quantity, and each metric comes from the analysis or from the measured
slew; the total mass enters through the launch cost, the MoE on it, which the launch-budget
requirement reads; two requirements read quantities that are not traded (the available power, the
peak momentum)". Against the adopted model's view: the slew time and its two requirements are gone,
the four dashed "no requirement" boxes are gone, the marks are new, the launch-cost box is new.
