The two reports below are the records of the round-1 cold reads (D-10, R-8): a fresh P2 proxy
(practising engineer, depth L-B) and a fresh P3 proxy (systems modeller, depth L-C), each in its own
context, each reading the image before the caption, on the folder as round 1 left it
(`assembly_view.png`, `behavioural_view.png`, `heatmap.png`, `coverage.md`, `k3_record.md`,
`INTENT.md`, `README.md`). The reports are quoted as written and are not edited; the readers' own
words ("facet", "stock", "weight") stand. After each report, a table gives each finding's
disposition and where it was executed: a round-3 commit (`abdb88f`, the views by the round-1
dispositions, generator revision 4; `a93ce10`, the interrogation of the three metrics that never
bind) or a round-4 commit (`43e1a7f`, the round-3 dispositions, the pair's regime names), with the

drawing, which belongs to `case.tau_sweep_figure` (read-only for WP23; the list is `WP23-review.md`,

reason; or "open" where the record holds no disposition (listed for the PM in the review,
"Rounds: the cold reads and their dispositions").

15. [note] As P2: the view convinces me on bookkeeping (every traded metric has one requirement and one mark, and I can count 7 MoP and 2 MoE) but not that it is a trade study, because no requirement shows a threshold, unit or direction, and each requirement-metric line is a 1:1 renaming ("the shared quantity on the line"), so the picture cannot say what trades against what or which requirements bind.

17. [note] Questions I would ask the modeller: what is each requirement's threshold and is it satisfied by most designs; why is pointing error an MoE and settling time an MoP; is launch cost a monotone function of total mass (then it is a renaming, not a second quantity) or does it add anything; why are available power and peak momentum computed but not traded when two requirements read them; and what does "field data when fielded" commit the measured-slew metrics to that the computed ones are not.

# Round 1, P3 proxy (systems modeller, depth L-C), fresh context, image before caption. Verbatim.

Cold sentences (image alone, before any text):

- behavioural_view.png: "Nine orange requirement boxes in three subsystem lanes (controls; power and energy; mass) each trace by an orange line to one green metric box tagged MoP or MoE, seven of the metrics are fed by a lilac SlewExperiment and two by a grey SlewMeasurement, launch cost is a yellow MoE sitting between a requirement and total mass, and two requirements (power, momentum) in a dashed box at the top trace to nothing." Boxes: orange = requirement def with satisfy/verify stereotypes; green = metric (MoP, one MoE); yellow = derived MoE; lilac = analysis that computes; grey = measurement. Lines: orange = requirement reads the labelled quantity; grey = where the metric's value comes from. Dashed = orphaned.
- assembly_view.png: "A Cubesat part def is composed of eight parts (bus, solar panel, battery, antenna, IMU, reaction wheel, star tracker, controller), two of which are swapped among three alternatives (wheel class, tracker grade) and four of which carry a continuously tuned parameter (panel span, battery capacity, Kp, Kd), so there are six design variables and the untagged parts are fixed." Filled diamonds = composition; hollow triangles = specialisation of an abstract part; orange tag = categorical swap; blue tag = continuous tune; the legend lists levels and ranges.
- heatmap.png: "As a temperature tau goes from 3 to infinity, the fraction of designs above V = 1 and the effective sample size both fall steeply, a grey bar near tau 40 marks where the fraction crosses the 0.1 line, and the grey grid below shows some per-metric weight (0 to 0.3) is dominated by total mass (solid black) with energy balance, depth of discharge and pointing error next, settling time fading as tau grows, and power margin, eclipse reserve and peak slew power at zero throughout." Top-left line = fraction vs tau with a threshold line; top-right = effective sample size; grey bar = chosen tau; grid rows = metrics, columns = tau values, shade = an unnamed weight; colourbar 0 to 0.3.

Findings:

2. [changes the view] heatmap.png, grid: the selected temperature (grey bar) is marked on the two curves but not on the grid where the exponents are read, so the reader cannot find the tau 38.1 column (columns 25 and 42 bracket it) without the record.
3. [changes the view] heatmap.png, colourbar and axis labels: the shade is never named (no "exponent w" on the colourbar), "V" is undefined in the image, the 0.1 line is unlabelled, and the x-axis is "temperature tau" on top and "tau" below; a cold reader cannot say what the grid measures.
4. [changes the view] INTENT.md: it states an intent for the two views and coverage.md but none for heatmap.png, so test 1 (the written takeaway compared to the cold read) cannot be run on the heatmap; README.md's "K = 1 exponent heatmap across tau" is the nearest statement.
5. [changes the view] README.md "A finding for the record" versus k3_record.md's refusal text: the two refusals name "facet 1" (K = 1 band) and "facet 0" (K = 3 band) but the README attributes both to "term 1"; either the facet index is 0-based in one place and 1-based in the other or one attribution is wrong.
6. [changes the view] Two datasets, five names: assembly_view.png legend "baseline gains / aggressive gains", README.md "primary (aggressive gain box) / baseline (stock gain box)", k3_record.md "primary regime" then "stock regime" (its first line says "baseline regime"), filenames `heatmap.png` / `heatmap_stock.png` and `..._gains.csv` / `....csv`; and behavioural_view.png's "«satisfy» cubesatBaseline" uses "baseline" in a third sense (a configuration, not a dataset).
7. [changes the view] behavioural_view.png, colour versus stereotype: green carries both «MoP» boxes and the «MoE» pointing error, while yellow carries the «MoE» launch cost; on the cold read I took green = MoP and yellow = MoE, and only the legend's seventh line (colour = traded versus derived, stereotype = MoE/MoP) corrected it.
8. [changes the view] behavioural_view.png, legibility: at 700 px the «satisfy»/«verify» lines and the whole legend are 6 to 7 px text, unreadable at column width without zooming; the intent's "one look" depends on the legend being read.
9. [changes the view] assembly_view.png versus INTENT.md: the intent says "a cubesat of nine parts" but the drawing has eight part boxes; the ninth is hidden in SolarPanel's parenthetical "(panelPlus, panelMinus)", which a one-look reader does not count.
10. [note] assembly_view.png, legend "Gains Kp and Kd: one range per dataset": the intent's "that is the whole configuration space" is two configuration spaces differing in the Kp box; the view does not say which one any figure that follows uses.
11. [note] assembly_view.png: untagged parts (Bus, Antenna, Imu) are read as fixed by inference only; the legend keys swap and tune and never says what no tag means.
12. [note] behavioural_view.png versus coverage.md prose: coverage says the analysis "passes through" the two measured attributes, while the view draws SlewMeasurement feeding settling time and control effort directly and SlewExperiment "computes 7 of the 9", so the two records disagree on whether the measured metrics go through the analysis.
13. [note] behavioural_view.png versus coverage.md: total mass and pointing error are drawn as SlewExperiment outputs but are the two verified in ConfigurationVerification (all other analysis outputs are SlewVerification), and coverage calls total mass an "attribute"; the view does not distinguish pass-through attributes from computed returns among the seven lilac lines.
14. [note] behavioural_view.png, idiom: «satisfy» cubesatBaseline and «verify» ... drawn as stereotype lines inside a «requirement def» box borrows the UML-stereotype idiom for what SysML v2 models as relationships (satisfy, verification case) on usages; to a v2 reader it also reads as the claim that the baseline configuration satisfies all nine requirements, which the record does not state or check.
15. [note] behavioural_view.png, arrows: the orange arrowheads (requirement to metric, a trace or read) and the grey arrowheads (source to metric, a flow) point in opposite semantic directions with the same head; the cold read took both as flow.
16. [note] coverage.md, "Traded metrics" sentence: the stated order (measured pass-throughs first, then the analysis's returns) does not match its own row order, where control effort (measured) is fourth after two calcs.
17. [note] Two names for one object across the views: line labels in the model's identifiers (settlingTime, PointingError, LaunchCost) against box labels in words (settling time, pointing error, launch cost), and requirement names on two patterns (SettledRequirement reads settlingTime; LaunchBudgetRequirement reads LaunchCost; the rest are <Metric>Requirement).
18. [note] README.md and k3_record.md, "5-lemma": the count noun "lemma" for a contending set is unexplained in the round record and is not a term a modeller at L-C can resolve from these four files.
19. [note] heatmap.png versus coverage.md (consistency, not physics): the three metrics at zero exponent across tau include the two columns (power margin, peak slew power) that carry every "structural yes / empirical no" star in the coverage table; eclipse reserve has none; the record does not say whether this coincidence was looked at.
20. [note] Consistency checks that pass: the behavioural view's nine requirements, nine metrics, MoE/MoP marks, line labels and verification cases agree with coverage.md's second table; the assembly view's two swaps and four tunes equal coverage.md's six decision elements; the heatmap's nine rows equal the coverage table's traded metrics in the same order; the grey bar, the fraction just under 0.1, the n_eff near 20 and the zero-exponent list (power margin, eclipse reserve, peak slew power) agree with k3_record.md's primary K = 1 line.
