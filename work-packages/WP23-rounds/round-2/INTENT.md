## assembly_view.png

After one look a reader says: "a configuration is a cubesat of nine parts; two of them are swapped
for one of three alternatives (the wheel class, the tracker grade) and four numbers are tuned within
their ranges (panel span, battery capacity, the gains Kp and Kd); that is the whole configuration
space". The PlantUML text is byte for byte round 1's: round 2 adds no configuration variable (the
battery stays a tuned capacity; its rating is a constant of the environment).

## behavioural_view.png

After one look a reader says: "every traded metric, in its subsystem, is an MoE or an MoP that one
requirement reads by its own quantity, and each metric comes from the analysis or from the measured
slew; the total mass enters through the launch cost, the MoE on it, which the launch-budget
requirement reads; three requirements read quantities that are not traded (the available power, the
peak momentum, and the pack's peak power against the eclipse peak load)". Against round 1's view the
one change is the third box in the dashed group at the top, `BatteryPeakPowerRequirement`
(satisfied by the baseline, verified in `SlewVerification`): it bounds the eclipse peak load by the
pack's rating and so reads no traded metric directly, like the power requirement.
