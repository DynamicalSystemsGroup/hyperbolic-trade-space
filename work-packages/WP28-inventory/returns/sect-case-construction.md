# sect-case-construction

## Q1. What the section conveys

It builds the cubesat case that the story's last result reads: the design variables (Figure 2), the eight attributes and the requirements that read them (Figure 3), the two regimes and their feasible counts (706 of 1000, 780 of 1000), the conditioning, and how the fit temperature is picked for the K = 1 fit (Figure 4, tau = 38.1).

## Q2. Which sentence of the story it serves

Main result 7 ("On a cubesat model coupling power, mass and attitude control the terms separate contending sets that one term blends"). It also serves the abstract's closing sentence ("On a cubesat model coupling power, mass and attitude control ...") and the Contribution paragraph's count of 706 feasible designs on 4 and 8 attributes.

## Q3. Verdict

advances. Result 7 and the abstract's last sentence need a case whose variables, attributes, feasibility and conditioning are stated, and this is the only section in the paper body that states them. Section 9 reads the case and Appendix C gives the model in detail, but neither stands in for this setup as the reader meets it. Some parts carry less weight. The centring clause (equations (1) and (2) at K = 1) is about the fit, not the case. The closing paragraph on the planted-term generator (900 rows, 20 tables of 400 rows) is not tied to anything else in the section.
