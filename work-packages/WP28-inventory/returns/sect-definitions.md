# sect-definitions

## Q1. What the section conveys

It gives the paper's working definitions (Definitions 2 to 9): the invariant value V and its log as the energy differential, the posynomial sum of terms, the two temperatures and the near-frontier band, the chart (Z, log V), the n-lemma of a term, reach and share, elasticity, and the ascent direction.

## Q2. Sentence of the story served

Main-results item 1 (the invariant value, the surface where it equals one, position and level, elasticities, "Definition 1") and item 2 (exponents are elasticities, the chart is reversible). Also the abstract sentences "A reversible chart separates a design's position along the surface from its energy differential" and "Each monomial term ... reports its contending metrics, an n-lemma ... with elasticities".

## Q3. Verdict

advances. The story uses the terms energy differential, chart, n-lemma, elasticity, band and posynomial, and this is the place where each gets a precise form (a formula or a rule), which Section 1 and the abstract only name by analogy. Not every definition is needed by the story (see Q6).

## Q6. Boxed statements

- Definition 2 (Invariant form): advances. Gives V as a weighted geometric mean, scaled to 1 on the surface; the story's invariant value.
- Definition 3 (Posynomial): advances. The several-term model; "a piece of the surface, not a set of designs" matches Section 1.
- Definition 4 (Temperatures and band): advances. Fit and term temperature and the near-frontier band; the band and the soft support are in the story.
- Definition 5 (The chart): advances. The reversible rewriting (Z, log V) of the abstract and of item 2.
- Definition 6 (The n-lemma): advances. The contending set is how the abstract's n-lemma is found; Figure 1 illustrates it.
- Definition 7 (Reach and share): reach advances (it scales V to 1 on the surface); the term share does not advance, since no story sentence uses it.
- Definition 8 (Elasticity): advances. The percent-per-percent trade-off in the abstract and item 1.
- Definition 9 (Ascent direction): does not advance. No sentence of the abstract or Section 1 mentions tangent space or gradient of log V.
