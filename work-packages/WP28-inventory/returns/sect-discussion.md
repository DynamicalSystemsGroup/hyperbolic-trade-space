## Q1. What does this section convey?

It sets the limits on what the fit's output may be taken to mean: the certificate shows that the optimizer reached a local minimizer. Whether the surface describes the table, whether it holds beyond the table, and whether a reading is an explicable trade-off are separate claims left to human validation. Around this core it also reports a few case outcomes: requirements imposed before or after the fit, and the propose, predict, check trial.

## Q2. Which sentence of the story does it serve?

The abstract's opening sentence ("an answer and a justification an accountable executive can follow"), with Section 1's "the justification is the hard product". Smaller parts touch item 5 (the PCA contrast), item 6 (open questions) and item 1 (novelty read through Theorem 1).

## Q3. Verdict and reason

**advances.** The story promises a justification an executive can follow and defend. That needs a plain statement of what the method's outputs do and do not support. The "Three claims" and "Verify and validate" paragraphs give that statement, and the story points to no other unit for it. Several of the later paragraphs do not carry this job. "Limits, in brief" points to Appendix E. "Novelty and white space" restates Theorem 1. "The kernel direction" belongs with the future-work section. "What propose, predict, check showed" reports an experiment the story never mentions. These paragraphs make the section longer without serving the story sentence.
