## Q1
It lists, family by family, the earlier methods this work resembles (Pareto, DEA, production frontiers, PCA, log-ratio analysis, geometric programming, MDAO, tradespace exploration and others), saying for each what it does, what is shared and what is different, and then reports how closely each citing sentence in the paper was checked against what the cited work says.

## Q2
Item 5 and the abstract's "Like PCA, our method is unsupervised feature engineering; the two answer different questions" are served only in part (the PCA entry). Otherwise the story's Section 1 never makes a prior-art claim of its own; the one pointer is the "G prior art" in its Organization line. The abstract's "We explain tensions" against PCA's "explains variance" is the nearest sentence, and the PCA entry carries it. For most of the appendix: none.

## Q3
Verdict: advances. The story invites the objection "this is just PCA, or Pareto, or DEA", and the register is the one place that answers it for each neighbour with a "why this is not just the neighbour" line; nothing else in the story carries that. The reading-pass half is checking of the paper's own citations rather than anything the story says, so the appendix advances on the strength of its first half.
