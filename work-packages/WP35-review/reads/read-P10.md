## 2. Own-words summary (written before re-reading)
Appendix H asks whether HoroPCA, a PCA variant for hyperbolic space, estimates the same thing as this work. The authors predicted in advance that PCA and HoroPCA follow where the table spreads most, while this work follows the upper edge of the table, and they wrote down what outcomes would count against that. They built synthetic tables where the edge is fixed and the spread changes, where the edge is rotated away from the generator's direction, and where the edge is removed. Counting how many of six tables each method got the edge direction right (cosine of 0.9 or more), this work got it in every table with an edge, PCA and HoroPCA only when the spread happened to line up with it. Every prediction held. The authors say the two methods answer different questions and can be chained, and list limits (their own embedding, clean synthetic data, six tables per setting).
