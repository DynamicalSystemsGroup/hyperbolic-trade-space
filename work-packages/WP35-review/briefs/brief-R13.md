# Brief: R-13, the cold reader without a persona, fresh read of Section 2 and Appendix H

Common rules, placeholders, findings format and checks C1 to C7: `common.md` (read it first, it is binding).
Fill-ins from the PM: `{STAGE_URL}`, `{HEAD}`, `{OUT_PATH}`, `{SHOT_DIR}`.

## Persona

A technically literate reader (a graduate engineer or data scientist) who has not heard of this work or of HoroPCA.
You have no stake in either. You are told nothing about what the pages are meant to say; the point of this read is what
they say to you.

## What to read

1. Section 2 of the paper (`{STAGE_URL}/paper/`), the HoroPCA paragraph and what surrounds it for context.
2. Appendix H in full (`{STAGE_URL}/paper/supporting-material/`, "Appendix H"). Appendix G only as far as its pointer
   to H.

## Questions (answer in your own words first, then quote)

Write the first three answers before you reread anything. This is the read the PM compares, word for word, with the
intended reading; do not tune your words to what you think is wanted.

1. What is HoroPCA, as these pages describe it?
2. How does this work differ from it?
3. What do the two share, and where do they part?
4. Does any sentence imply that this work is a PCA, a hyperbolic-geometry method or a kernel method? Does any imply
   that HoroPCA is worse, poorer or inferior at what it was built for? Quote any sentence that comes near either, and
   say what you took from it.
5. State the experiment's hypothesis, what would have refuted it, and what the result was, from the appendix alone.
6. State, from the appendix alone, what the experiment does not show. Compare with the reading limits and quote any
   claim that goes past them.
7. Is every number you were asked to rely on given a source or command? List any that has none.
8. Navigation: from Section 2's paragraph can you find the appendix in one step? From the table, can you reach its
   source? Do the links resolve? Console or page errors?

## Additional checks

C1 to C7 of `common.md`. This read is the acceptance read for the register (C6) and the reading limits (C7): give
those two extra care, quoting the sentences that settle them.

## Not a balance read

You are not asked to weigh precision against clarity (that is the P9 and P10 pair's job). Report what you understood,
what you did not, and what a sentence made you believe that the experiment does not support.
