# WP70 D-2 — Open data for the iron triangle: the survey

Every row was read on 2026-11-16 (the date read, for all rows). Every link was resolved on that date (R-1; the check
is the last section). "Sides" names which of the three sides of the triangle the data carries and how: **T** schedule
(a plan and an actual, or the slip directly), **B** budget (likewise), **S** scope (story points, function points, or
the dataset's own measure). "Quality" is the m quality measures. "Count" is how many of the 3 + m the data carries at
the grain named; the table is ranked by it, ties broken by the grain's row count and then by the ease of the fetch.

## What the ranking says

- Two open tables carry three of the 3 + m at a usable grain: TAWOS (schedule, scope and quality per sprint, no budget)
  and Kitchenham-CSC (all three sides per project, no quality). None of the open sources found carries all three sides
  and a quality measure at one grain.
- The agile archives carry scope and quality and the time box, and not money: a sprint's time and cost are fixed by
  design and its scope floats, so the triangle in a sprint archive is mostly a scope-and-quality question.
- The public portfolios (UK, Québec, World Bank, US) carry cost and time and a rating, with the plan either missing
  from a single year's file or held only as successive snapshots; the slip is recovered by joining years, which is a
  study's work, not a download.
- The construction and infrastructure collections with real overrun data (the megaproject databases behind the
  cost-overrun literature) were not found open; only cost levels (Transit Costs) are.

## How the links were checked

`curl -L` returned 200 for each landing page or file on 2026-11-16, except the publishers' DOI pages (ACM, IEEE,
Elsevier), which refuse scripted requests; every DOI cited resolves in the DOI handle API (`doi.org/api/handles/`,
response code 1) to the record named. The Zenodo, figshare and GitHub APIs returned
each record's files, sizes and licence (the restricted Jira records return no files). The GMPP CSV, the
Québec CSV of 4 September 2025 and the Kitchenham ARFF were downloaded and read; the Kitchenham file is the case
study's file of record (`case/manifest.toml`). Row counts marked "(paper)" or "(records)" are the source's own.
