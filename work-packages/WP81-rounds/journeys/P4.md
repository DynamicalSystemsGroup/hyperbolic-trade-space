argument for the method" and say what the paper is now: a working paper whose abstract is published. If the abstract
page stays, call it that: "the paper's abstract (working paper)". For me the pointer can simply go, since the home
page already gives me a use path. (d) The "project page" sentence is a site map, not documentation for a user. (e)
"Posynomial invariant form" is not defined until later, and "frontier" has no ML meaning for me. The last line, "PCA
finds where the data spread, this finds where they stop, and the two chain", is the best sentence on the page. It is
buried at the bottom, and "chain" is not shown. Is it a Pipeline? In which order?

**2. Overview.** (a) I wanted the PCA comparison made concrete. (b) I found no paper pointer on this page. (c)
Nothing to change. (d) Nothing here belongs to the paper. (e) The PCA paragraph is good: it says "trade-offs, not
