**My take.** 1. Yes: install, first fit and the printed numbers are all on Getting started and work as code. I could
read the exponents (`[0.3 0.37 0.33]`) and log V. 2. I stopped at `temperature`'s meaning, and at the Termination

and the argument. For them the one paper pointer on home is the useful one, but today it lands on only the abstract,
