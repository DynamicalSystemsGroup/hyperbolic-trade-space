## Sensitivity of the conclusions to the step-quantized times

Generated by `examples/adcs/experiments/sensitivity.py` (WP16 Amendment 1, D-6); no Docker.

**Method.** Each of the two cubesat datasets (primary, aggressive gain box; baseline, stock gain box) and the six check designs were regenerated 5 times through the clean oracle (`CubesatOracle`; the sampler, design ids and seeds of the v2 generation; the same worker pool) with `Kp`, `Kd`, `panel_span_m` and `battery_wh` of every design multiplied by `1 + e`, `e` uniform in [-1e-12, 1e-12] per design and variable from `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)`, seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. The regenerated files were written to a scratch directory (never `examples/adcs/data`, never the ledger). For each regeneration `case.py` was run as the paper runs it: the one-term fit with the temperature re-selected, the K = 2 and K = 3 fits (`spec.seed`), the regime comparison, the nondominated counts, the candidates and propose-predict-check (the regressor refit on the regenerated primary data; the recorded predictions; the check designs regenerated with the same kind of perturbation, and, as a second reading, the committed check measurements). Every table of Appendix D and every generated sentence of the case sections was computed, and each quantity is compared at the precision the paper prints it (tables at four significant digits, temperatures at one decimal, sentences verbatim). The terms of K = 2 and K = 3 are compared as labelled and, separately, as an unlabelled set.
