## With the caption

What the caption settles:
- The figure is the K = 2 fit, and each dot is a design's log V of that fit, plotted against its position along the surface.
- V is the invariant value, defined in the document at def-invariant.
- The position is the first principal direction of the near-frontier designs, taken from the K = 1 fit. It is a description of the sampled designs, not a model variable. The 36% is that direction's share of the near-frontier spread.
- The surface is the flat line log V = 0 at every position, by construction.
- Grey is the near-frontier band.
- Above the line means V > 1, beyond the surface. Below means V < 1, inside.
- About a tenth of the designs lie above the line, which the caption calls the fit's convention.
