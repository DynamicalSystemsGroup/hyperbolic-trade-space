hyperbolic-trade-space

Posynomial invariant form: the shape of a design trade space, learned from evaluated designs

Michael Zargham

A design study evaluates many configurations and gets back a handful of numbers for each: mass, power, settling time, pointing error. Some designs win on one number and lose on another. This project models the edge of what is achievable as a posynomial frontier, and reads from it the tensions between the metrics, the exchange rates between them, and the gaps where no design has yet been tried. Every result is tied to a SymPy-checked derivation, and the package follows the scikit-learn conventions.

The paper tells the method through a spacecraft attitude-control trade study, the documentation explains and proves it page by page, and the package is the tool you run on your own data. Start wherever suits you.
