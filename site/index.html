hyperbolic-trade-space

An unsupervised learning method which applies a posynomial invariant form, a sum of terms each a product of powers of the metrics, to learn the shape of a trade space from evaluated designs.

Michael Zargham

Adopting it: the analyst writes the conditioning; from install to a first fit took 6 s, or about 100 s counting the reading and the writing of the script, for readers new to it; few designs determine the exponents only weakly. The cubesat case is a synthetic demonstrator.

Why

Trade studies are usually decided from documents and tables, where the detail of the evaluated designs is lost, and the alternatives are bespoke or opaque. A model-based method has to clear a bar before a decision-maker can trust it: it has to show its reasoning, say where the data do not reach, and let the analysis be rerun.

What

PCA explains variance; this explains tensions. Its unit is the n-lemma, a power-weighted product of the metrics that names which of them compete on one part of the trade space. The method belongs in unsupervised learning and feature engineering beside PCA: kin rather than a substitute, and the two chain (the tension report already runs PCA on the near-frontier chart coordinates). It ships as a scikit-learn-compatible estimator (fit, transform, inverse_transform, score_samples; the conformance checks pass), so it drops into the same workflows.
