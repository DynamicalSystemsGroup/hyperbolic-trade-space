History and acknowledgements

This page acknowledges the people and the human lineage of this work, which the paper’s citations cannot carry. In our own account, it came not from an academic group but from a contract research and engineering firm, founded in 2017 as BlockScience and now the Dynamical Systems Group. We perform requirements, architecture, design, simulation and analysis for clients, all software-defined, studying the spaces of feasible designs of their systems under uncertain, strategic and adversarial users.

Before the firm: Zargham’s convex optimization (2009, 2014) and, with Victor Preciado and others, geometric programs for protecting networks against spreading processes (2014; 354 citations on Google Scholar, 12 November 2026), in the form of Boyd et al. (2007) that this method’s frontier takes. With Preciado and Zixuan Zhang came the 2020 journal version of the 2018 state-space framework, which applies control engineering to economic networks.

cadCAD was implemented by Joshua Jodesty and designed by Michael Zargham, Markus Koch and Matthew Barlin (AUTHORS.txt), and extended by Danilo Bernardineli (a physicist, by his profile) and Emanuel Lima. Jamsheed Shorish (trained in economics) co-wrote Generalized Dynamical Systems and presented it in video; Andrew “Octopus” Penland, a research engineer at BlockScience, led workshops on MAIDs, GDS and cadCAD; Kris Paruch (“a mathematical background with focus on economic modeling”) co-wrote Economic Games as Estimators. Rohan Mehta wrote gds-core and gds-axelrod; gds-core credits the MSML and bdp-lib of Sean McOwen. Andrew Clark, thanked in AUTHORS.txt, wrote with Alexander Mihailov and Zargham a community-currency model (2023) developed with the Red Cross and Grassroots Economics.

In our own account, Jakob Hackel and Carolina Riascos-Álvarez (CarolinaRiascos) worked on client projects we cannot name, and relevant work was done at the firm by people we thank but cannot describe here; in our own account of the team, its backgrounds are computer science, control, economics, physics and mathematics.

Special cases where the work was highly public: a review of two competing Filecoin proposals (2023), debated publicly on GitHub and Slack; and the RAI controller, a highly automated system, in its design record (2021) and in Zargham’s talk at a workshop in Edinburgh (ICMS), October 2024.

Zargham serves on the NumFOCUS Advisory Council, the firm is a community sponsor, and his PyData NYC 2023 and SciPy 2026 talks are public. The team works on and with OpenMBEE, Zargham as a maintainer across OpenSysML and Flexo MMS; in our own account, this project is part of a synthesis effort to make our learnings accessible.

With computers, fast but with confidence.
