The amniote life-history database (Myhrvold et al. 2015; doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.3563457.v1) is
released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); the licence is read from
the figshare record (2026-11-14). The Ecological Archives page (default.htm in the same record)
carries the line "Copyright Authors" and says nothing more on copyright.

Citation: Myhrvold et al., "An amniote life-history database to perform comparative analyses with
birds, mammals, and reptiles", Ecology 96:3109 (2015), doi:10.1890/15-0846R.1.

Nothing from the archive is committed to this repository. The archive is fetched at run time into
examples/amniote/data/ (ignored by git) and checked against manifest.toml.
