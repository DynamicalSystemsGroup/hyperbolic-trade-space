## Exclusion by the solver

The solver (engine `solve`, z3) classifies the regions of each v2 experiment's declared ranges before any design is simulated (`experiments/exclusion.py`); a region proved infeasible is excluded, any other is kept, and the four flags are then observations of the measured slew. The classification is stored in each entry's `inputs.exclusion`.

| entry | power region | battery region | excluded regions | variant excluded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2-primary@3 | all-feasible | mixed | none | False |
| v2-baseline@3 | all-feasible | mixed | none | False |
| v2-check-designs@3 | all-feasible | mixed | none | False |
| scope-sensitivity-shared-subspace-v2@2 | all-feasible | mixed | none | False |

- `design-space power` (exact accounting (PowerRequirement)): all-feasible; span_m in [0.3, 1], tracker_w in [0.4, 1.5]. every configuration in the region satisfies the power condition (proved). Witness of a violation: none.
- `design-space battery` (exact accounting (DepthOfDischargeRequirement), slew energy as stated): mixed; capacity_wh in [20, 200], slew_energy_wh in [0, 0], tracker_w in [0.4, 1.5]. the region has feasible and infeasible configurations; kept, each point is tested. Witness of a violation: {'capacity_wh': 20.0, 'slew_energy_wh': 0.0, 'tracker_w': 0.4}.

Result: the solver's verdicts on the declared ranges are all-feasible, mixed; 0 region(s) excluded, so every design of each dataset is simulated and the flags are observations. The standard space is not decided by the solver: the battery region is mixed (capacities below the eclipse base load floor fail the depth of discharge, the rest depend on the slew loss energy). The agreement of observation with verdict is tested on excluded sub-regions (`tests/test_cubesat_oracle.py`): a battery region of 20 to 26 Wh is proved infeasible and every design sampled in it simulates `dod_ok` False.
