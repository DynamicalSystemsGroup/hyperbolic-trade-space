What the eight answers show: 4 of 4 placed the tension in the model under either vocabulary; both A readers and one B reader wavered at the same place, the membership phrases ("the facet's rows", "facet k dominates"; in B "the designs whose largest term it is"), not at the noun. The A readers' image of the noun was "one face of a many-sided object", the right geometry; the B readers got the algebra ("a product of the metrics each raised to an exponent"), and the executive noted "nobody has told me what a monomial is". At passage level the cluster reading rides on the membership wording and the former colouring, and both vocabularies carry the model reading once the finding sentence is present.

One benefit the readers cannot show but the vocabulary rule does (`vocabulary-review.md`, rule 1): the paper today says "A term, a monomial and a facet are one object", three names for one object, kept apart by "facet for the geometry and term for the algebra". The rename leaves one name, with "cell" for the designs.

"Quilt" can serve once, where the Preliminaries now say the three names are one object: the fitted surface is a quilt of trade-offs, each monomial term one patch, flat in log space, meeting its neighbours at seams (the ridges the paper already names). The docsite index could carry the same sentence. It should not enter the glossary, a caption or a theorem, and "patch" should not become a noun for the term; the vocabulary tool passes "quilt" as plain words because it is no stem.

## 4. Options

Measured benefit, for all four: the reader grid attributes the cluster reading to the figure colouring (now redrawn) and the membership wording, not to the noun; the A/B found the same under both vocabularies. The benefits the evidence does not measure are the one-object-one-name rule and the author's framing (terms representing trade-offs; the model leading, the geometry following). A depth-B re-read of the redrawn build (one reader round, no code) would show whether the cluster reading survives WP19 without any rename.

1. **Keep "facet"; fix the membership sentences.** The remaining "facet's rows", "designs facet k dominates", "rows assigned to each facet" become cell wording (CR-3's open item); the polyhedral sentences stay. Effort: under 1 unit (the paper, a few register fragments, test pins). Benefit: removes the phrases every A/B waverer named. Blast radius: the paper and its register.
2. **Prose and glossary now; public names after 0.1.** Paper, supporting-material prose and table headers, docs, site, glossary, derivation step text with Appendix B, captions, register fragments, phrase pins. Effort: 3 to 4 units plus a container regeneration. Benefit: one name in everything a reader reads. Blast radius: every prose surface; the code and the API reference keep `facet_share_` and `assign_facets` beside "term share" and "cell" until the API moves, a seam the docstrings bridge (as `max_gap` is bridged today).
3. **Everything, API included, in one package before 0.1.** Adds `src`, the contract amendment (non-additive), tests, examples, figure labels, the generated artefacts. Effort: 6 to 8 units plus the container regeneration and image rebuild. Benefit: a 0.1 with no name a reader must translate and no deprecation ever owed. Blast radius: every surface; lands across the files of the three executing packages, so it is sequenced after them or they rebase.
4. **Prose now; the API as its own package before 0.1.** Option 2, then the API part of option 3, two gates. Effort: 4 + 3 units. Benefit: as option 3. Blast radius: split; option 2's seam exists between the two packages.

The author decides.
